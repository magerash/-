## A) Tim Urban / Wait But Why
**"Your Life in Weeks" (May 2014)** — each box = one week; canonical grid = **90 × 52 =
4,680 boxes**; each row = one year. Emotional payload: a whole life on one sheet makes
finitude tangible. Companion variations ("life in months") + a physical Life Calendar
poster (~24″×36″). https://waitbutwhy.com/2014/05/life-weeks.html

**"The Tail End" (Dec 2015)** — the strongest inspiration for the "remaining time"
angle. Converts a life into remaining *units of experience* (summers, books, times you
see your parents). Gut-punch: by the time you move out you've used **~93% of your
in-person time with your parents**. Reframes planning around *whom/what you spend
remaining boxes on*.

**Procrastination framework** — "Why Procrastinators Procrastinate" (Oct 2013), "How to
Beat Procrastination" (Nov 2013), "The Procrastination Matrix" (Mar 2015):
- **Rational Decision-Maker (RDM)** — adult brain, plans for the future.
- **Instant Gratification Monkey** — lives in the present, no memory/future, wants only
  "easy and fun"; seizes the wheel.
- **Dark Playground** — distraction *when you should be working*; the fun isn't real —
  it's saturated with guilt/anxiety/dread.
- **Panic Monster** — dormant until a **deadline** looms (the only thing the Monkey
  fears); wakes → Monkey flees → RDM regains control (why work happens last-minute).
- Sequel adds the *productive* geography: Critical Entrance, Dark Woods, Flow, Happy
  Playground. **Key product insight ("Matrix"):** the Panic Monster only fires on
  deadlines, so **deadline-less life goals get postponed forever** — exactly the gap a
  planning tool fills.
Distilled into the TED talk "Inside the Mind of a Master Procrastinator" (2016).

## B) Market & positioning
**Oliver Burkeman, _Four Thousand Weeks_ (2021)** — an ~80-yr life ≈ 4,000 weeks. Thesis:
stop fighting finitude; the "efficiency trap" means doing more just invites more →
consciously **choose what matters**. WBW makes finitude *felt*; Burkeman makes
deliberate-choice *the prescription*. Our product sits at that intersection.
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four_Thousand_Weeks:_Time_Management_for_Mortals

**Demand/trend:** validated and commercially proven — memento-mori **posters** are an
established Amazon/Etsy niche; framed Stoic versions sell at premium; digital tools
(lifeweeks.app; Bryan Braun's grid; open-source variants). But the space is
**fragmented and backward-looking** → our future-planning + compare-to-people angle is
underserved.

**Naming/SEO — taken/crowded (avoid):** *Your Life in Weeks* (owns the SEO),
*lifeweeks.app*, *4kweeks.com*, *Life in Weeks®* (registered poster trademark),
*LifeTime 4000 Weeks*, *Weeks of My Life*. Literal "life in weeks / 4000 weeks / memento
mori" terms are crowded and partly trademarked → **pick a distinctive name** on the boat
metaphor.

**Candidate names** (verify domain + trademark before committing): **Helm**, **Tiller**,
Steerage, Set Sail, **Waypoint**, **Headway**, Trimtab, Bearing, Lifeboat, Compass Weeks.
→ **User chose Waypoint** (see decisions D-001).

**Positioning statement:**
> *Steer your life's boat, don't drift down the river — see all your weeks at a glance,
> plan the ones ahead with intention, and measure your journey against the lives that
> inspire you.*
