# Research — Competitive Landscape (2026-06-22)

> Method: WebSearch + WebFetch. WebFetch was blocked (403) on dynamic/app pages, so app
> ratings/exact prices come from search snippets and are flagged medium confidence.

## Conceptual origin (anchor for the whole category)
- **Tim Urban / Wait But Why — "Your Life in Weeks" (2014).** The seminal post; sells a
  physical **24″×36″ Life Calendar poster for ~$20**. _High confidence._
  https://waitbutwhy.com/2014/05/life-weeks.html ·
  https://store.waitbutwhy.com/products/life-calendar
- **Oliver Burkeman — "Four Thousand Weeks" (2021).** Second major cultural driver;
  "~4,000 weeks" framing spawned many apps/tools.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four_Thousand_Weeks:_Time_Management_for_Mortals

## Web / interactive tools (closest competitors)
- **Bryan Braun "Your Life in Weeks"** (bryanbraun.com/your-life/weeks.html) — free
  interactive grid, widely cloned reference implementation.
- **weeksofyour.life** and **weeksoflife.com** — free; enter birthday, color-code "life
  chapters," mark milestones. Inspired by Tim Urban + Buster Benson. _Medium confidence._
- **lifeweeks.app** — free map-of-life builder; notably **auto-adds birthdays, world
  events, and curated collections**. Nearest existing thing to a "famous-people
  overlay," but it adds *events*, not other people's lifemaps.
- **ekn.io** — free generator producing a printable PDF.
- Many free GitHub/Figma/Chrome-extension variants. Category baseline: **free,
  privacy-friendly (local-only), low monetization.**

## Mobile apps (where monetization lives)
- **Lifetime: Life Progress** (iOS) — dots, % progress, widgets, "Memento Mori
  Reminder." **Paywalled** (subscription + lifetime pass). Top complaint: *"can't do
  anything without a paywall."* _Medium confidence on specifics._
- **Lifev — Life in Weeks/Calendar** (iOS) — premium = countdowns, goals, memories,
  chapters, timeline, insights, export/share, local-first.
- **4K Weeks** (iOS, 4kweeks.com) — weeks-left %, Stoic journal, AI Stoic insights,
  widgets; core features behind subscription.
- **Life Calendar: Visual Timeline**, **Weeks – Life Tracker**, **Life Calendar
  Journal** — visual timelines/journaling.
- **Android:** "Memento Mori Life Calendar" (~$4.88 paid, ~1K installs), "Memento Mori:
  Stoic Calendar," etc.
- **WeCroak** (adjacent) — 5 daily mortality quotes; free + **"Leap" $1.99/mo**.
  Complaints: quotes drifted off-theme. _High confidence._

## Physical products
Posters/prints from **Daily Stoic** (4,160-dot Memento Mori Calendar), **Stoic
Reflections**, **ArtMinder/WeekMinder** (engraved birch), **LifeTime 4000 Weeks**, plus
Etsy/Amazon. One-time purchase.

## Synthesis
**Table stakes:** birthday-driven week grid; past vs remaining shading + % lived;
color-coded "life chapters"; milestone markers; widgets; Stoic/memento-mori framing;
share/export. Web = free + local; apps = subscription; physical = one-time.

**Missing across the board:** (1) genuine **forward/future planning** — the future is
blank boxes, not plannable blocks; (2) **comparison to other people** (only
lifeweeks.app overlays world *events*); (3) goal/deadline math tied to week ranges;
(4) counter-anxiety design (the grid can feel bleak).

**Differentiation opportunities:**
1. **Block-planning by selecting start/end weeks** — commit a project/goal/sabbatical to
   a future range; auto-compute "X weeks, ends at age Y." *No incumbent does this.*
2. **Famous-people lifemap comparison** — "by my current age, they had…". *Unique.*
3. **"At my age, they had…" achievement benchmarking** (not just lifespan).
4. **Reframe future as opportunity, not doom** (plannable/colorable, not blank).
5. **Shareable, designed exports** (poster upsell pattern).
6. **Transparent, generous free tier** (undercut aggressive paywalls on goodwill).

**Caveats:** iOS app prices/ratings and the .com tools couldn't be fetched directly
(403); flagged medium confidence. Could **not confirm any competitor offers true future
block-planning or personal famous-people comparison** — strengthening those as
differentiators, but a hands-on app audit is advised before launch positioning.
